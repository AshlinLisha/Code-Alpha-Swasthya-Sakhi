🌸 Swasthya Sakhi (स्वास्थ्य सखी)

Your health. Your story. Your next step.

Swasthya Sakhi is a privacy-conscious digital health platform built for recHERsion’26 (PS1: Women’s Healthcare). It empowers women to track menstrual cycles, monitor daily symptoms, and generate structured health summaries to improve communication during doctor consultations.

📌 Problem & Impact
Conditions like PCOS, Endometriosis, and Thyroid disorders often take years to diagnose due to inconsistent symptom tracking and fragmented patient-doctor conversations. Swasthya Sakhi solves this by allowing users to log daily health data and convert those logs into clear, actionable summaries for healthcare providers.

✨ Features
* Client-Side Data Privacy: All user entries remain 100% private, saved strictly in the browser using `localStorage` with zero backend server tracking.
* Cycle & Symptom Tracking: Log period dates, flow intensity, pain scores, mood shifts, and lifestyle indicators.
* Rule-Based Clinical Insights: Generates clear, non-diagnostic symptom timelines designed to bridge the gap between patient reporting and doctor visits.
* WHO-Backed Health Education: Verified educational hub covering menstrual health, PCOS, Endometriosis, Thyroid management, and nutrition.
* Mental Wellbeing Tools: Includes an interactive 4-4-4 box breathing exercise and curated self-care guidance.
* Find Care Directory: Quick helper to search nearby hospitals, clinics, and health centers.
* Safety & Emergency Helper: Features instant SOS messaging and quick access to emergency services (112 India).
