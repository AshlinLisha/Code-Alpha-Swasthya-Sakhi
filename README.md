🌸 Swasthya Sakhi (स्वास्थ्य सखी)

Your Health. Your Story. Your Next Step.

A comprehensive, 100% offline, privacy-first digital healthcare companion empowering women across all life stages.
With 100% client-side data persistence (localStorage), users maintain complete ownership of their intimate health data without privacy or security risks.

📌 Project Overview

Swasthya Sakhi is a holistic web platform built to address critical gaps in women's health tracking and diagnostic communication. While traditional period trackers only focus on dates, Swasthya Sakhi bridges reproductive health, chronic condition management (PCOS, Endometriosis, Thyroid disorders), mental wellness, and emergency safety into a unified, secure experience.

✨ Key Features & Live Architecture

1. 🩸 Smart Cycle & Ovulation Tracker
Tracks start/end dates, flow intensity, and pain scores (0–10).
Calculates cycle history and estimates upcoming period windows using localized algorithms.
Supports historical data entry to build accurate long-term predictive baselines.

2. 📓 Symptom & Condition Journal (Digital Medical Diary)
Logs daily physical symptoms, mood states, and pain levels.
Allows users to attach custom Clinician Discussion Tags (e.g., PCOS, Thyroid, Irregularity).
Feeds continuous health logs directly into structured summaries for medical consultations.

3. 📊 Doctor-Ready Insights Generator
Transforms raw daily logs into clear, organized medical timelines.
Reduces recall bias during clinical appointments by providing objective pain and symptom trends.

4. 🧠 Mind & Mental Health Hub
Includes interactive 4-4-4 Box Breathing guides for acute anxiety and panic management.
Provides grounded self-care frameworks tailored to hormonal shifts.

5. 🚨 Safety & Emergency Suite (SOS 112)
One-tap emergency connectivity (integrated 112 services).
Quick access to safety protocols and local emergency guidance.

6. 🔒The Privacy-First & Offline Advantage
Zero Backend & Serverless: Operating completely in the user's browser, no personal health metrics, dates, or notes ever touch a remote server.
100% Offline Accessibility: Essential for users in low-connectivity or rural environments. Symptom logging, emergency tools, and wellness guides work seamlessly without an active internet connection.
Client-Side Data Sovereignty: Data is persisted locally via localStorage. Zero third-party tracking scripts, zero cloud database reliance, and zero data selling.

🤖 1. Sakhi AI Assistant
Symptom Contextualization: Multilingual AI chatbot providing WHO-aligned health context for logged symptoms.
Journal Pattern Analysis: Intelligent insights identifying symptom trends over 30/60/90 days to flag for doctor visits.

💉 2. Injection & Medication Management Tracker

Standardized Screeners: Integration of PHQ-9, GAD-7, and Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) tools.

👩‍🦳 4. Inclusive Lifetime Care Hub
Maternal Care: Trimester guidance, kick counters, and postpartum recovery tracking.

Menopause Support: Hot flash loggers, bone density tracking, and hormone replacement therapy info.
