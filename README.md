🌸 Swasthya Sakhi
Your Health. Your Story. Your Next Step.

A comprehensive, 100% offline, privacy-first digital healthcare companion empowering women across all life stages.


📌 Project Overview

Swasthya Sakhi is a client-side Progressive Web App (PWA) built to address critical gaps in women's health tracking and diagnostic communication. Moving beyond standard date-only period trackers, Swasthya Sakhi integrates cycle forecasting, chronic condition monitoring (PCOS, Endometriosis, Anemia, Thyroid health), daily private journaling, mental wellness, offline relaxation tools, and emergency safety into a seamless, secure platform.
With 100% client-side data persistence (localStorage), users maintain complete ownership of their intimate health data with zero privacy or tracking risks.

🔒 Privacy-First & 100% Offline Architecture

Zero Backend & Serverless: Operating entirely within the user's browser, no personal health records, journals, or notes ever touch a remote server.
100% Offline Accessibility: Designed for low-connectivity and rural environments. All core tools, loggers, sound generators, games, and health guides work seamlessly without an active internet connection.
Client-Side Data Sovereignty: Data is persisted locally in localStorage. Zero third-party tracking scripts, zero cloud databases, and zero data telemetry.

✨ Feature Breakdown by Tab

1. 🏠 Home Tab (Dashboard)
 
Personalized Greeting & Metrics: Displays user welcome banner (Welcome ash 🌸), offline save status, and real-time metric counters (Entries, Water, Reminders).
Water & Diet Tracker: Track daily water intake (Litres e.g., 2) and iron-rich foods (e.g., Spinach) with instant daily totals.
Symptom Hint Checker: Interactive lookup tool allowing users to enter symptoms (e.g., fatigue, lump, discharge) for quick educational hints.

2. 🩸 Cycle Tab (Cycle Tracking)
 
Cycle Entry Form: Log period dates, flow intensity (Light, Medium, Heavy), mood states (Mood Happy/Sad), and physical symptoms.
Local Persistence & Entry Management: Displays recorded entries under Your Entries - With Delete with full local deletion control.

3. 📓 Journal Tab (Daily Private Journal)
 
Private Journaling Workspace: Safe space to express feelings with the prompt "Write feelings – private, stays in this phone only."
Mood Tagging: Attach mood tags (😊 Happy, etc.) to daily reflections.
Local Storage Log: Saved entries remain stored strictly inside the browser under Your Journal Entries.

4. 📚 Learn Tab (Health Education Hub)
 
Breast Cancer: Guidance on monthly self-exams post-period and early detection protocols.
Cervical Cancer & HPV: Education on HPV vaccination for ages 9–26 and free government hospital access.
Anemia: Dietary advice covering spinach, jaggery, beetroot, ragi, and iron tablets.
Pregnancy Care: Antenatal care (ANC) within 12 weeks, folic acid, iron supplements, and emergency contact guidance.

5. 🧠 Mind Tab (Mental Health & Wellness Suite)
   
Breathing Exercise: Interactive 1-minute box breathing visualizer (Start 1 min).
Relaxation Sounds (Offline Works): Audio relaxation player with ambient sounds (Rain, Ocean, White Noise, and Stop).
Bubble Pop Game: Interactive stress-relief minigame with real-time scoring (Score: 0).

6. ⏰ Medicine Tab
    
Reminder Creator: Configure tablet names (e.g., Iron), doses (e.g., 1 daily after food), disease context (e.g., Anemia), and specific alarm times (--:--).
Active Reminders Display: Organized overview of saved reminders under Your Reminders.

7. 🚨 SOS Tab
   
One-Tap Direct Calling: Direct speed-dial triggers to national helplines.
